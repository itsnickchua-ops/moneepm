Fund Updates: clearer context for Mari Invest customers

Fund Updates is a proposed feature that gives customers plain-language, sourced context on market developments that may have affected a fund. Customers see the same reviewed updates before they invest, in My Holdings and in a monthly summary. This demo uses Mari Invest Singapore Equity.

Concept only, not affiliated with MariBank or Monee. Market events are real (25 to 30 Sep 2026). Fund prices, returns and holding values are illustrative.
